Privacy Policy

PressureLine Support · 日本語 · 한국어

PressureLine has no account, no server of its own, no advertising and no analytics or tracking. The developer receives none of your data.

Location (for the forecast)

  • With your permission (While Using the App only), PressureLine gets your location to request the pressure forecast for where you are.
  • Before the request, the coordinate is rounded to two decimal places (about 1 km). Only this rounded coordinate is sent, and only to Apple’s WeatherKit service. Apple states that the location used for WeatherKit is not associated with personal identifiers and is not used to track you across requests.
  • When GPS altitude is accurate to 20 m, it is used on the device to set your elevation. The altitude is not sent anywhere.
  • The last rounded coordinate is saved on your iPhone so the app knows when you have moved about 25 km.
  • If you do not allow location, the app still records the barometer; only the forecast is missing.

Barometer readings (Motion & Fitness)

  • PressureLine reads your iPhone’s pressure sensor and stores readings on this iPhone for 90 days, in the app’s own storage shared only with its widgets.
  • Readings never leave your iPhone unless you export them as CSV; the file then goes only to the app or person you choose in the share sheet.

Notifications

Alerts are local notifications created on your iPhone. No push service is used.

Deleting your data

Settings › Data › Delete all readings removes all readings, the cached forecast and the saved location and elevation. Deleting the app removes all of its data. Whether data is included in device backups such as iCloud Backup follows your iOS settings.

Purchases

The paid download is handled by the App Store; the developer receives no payment details.

App Store privacy label

Coarse location, used for app functionality (the forecast), not linked to you and not used for tracking.
