What does Pitchback do?

Pitchback plays a note, an interval or a short melody. You sing it back and see how close you were: a score out of 100, how many cents sharp or flat, and whether your tone was steady. It starts with a 30-second range check, adapts the drills to what you miss, and shows note names in your system. The core is free; one optional one-time purchase unlocks the rest. There is no subscription, account or advertising.

How to use it

  1. Range check: on first launch tap Continue, allow the microphone, then Start. Slide your voice from your lowest comfortable note to your highest for 8 seconds, then sing back three notes. Pitchback sets your comfortable range and starting level. You can run it again from Home (Check again) or Settings.
  2. Today's practice: tap Start practice. Listen to the reference; after a short high cue it is your turn. Sing on any vowel. Your pitch is drawn live, and the result shows the score, the cents offset and steadiness. Use Hear answer, Try again or Next.
  3. Focus on: practise one area only: single notes, intervals up, intervals down or melodies. Melodies are scored note by note and you can sing them at your own pace.
  4. Pitch monitor: sing or hum to see the note name, cents offset, frequency and a scrolling trace.
  5. Settings: note names, sharps or flats, reference sound (Keys, Soft, Reed), concert pitch A4 from 415 to 446 Hz, Accept other octaves, show the target line while singing, exercises per session (5, 8 or 12), latency measurement and Reset progress.

How scoring works

  • Within 10 cents of the target is Excellent, within 25 Good, within 50 Almost (100 cents = one semitone).
  • The start of each sung note is skipped so a scoop into the note does not count. A regular vibrato is not counted as unsteady; slow drift is.
  • Intervals are judged from the note you heard. With Accept other octaves on, a single note or melody sung an octave higher or lower counts as correct.
  • Silence is not counted as a wrong answer and does not change your progress.

Note names

C D E (English), German (H and B, Helmholtz octaves), fixed Do (Do Re Mi … Si), Japanese ドレミ or ハニホ, Korean 도레미 or 다라마, and Indian sargam (Sa Re Ga with your chosen Sa). The default follows your device language; change it in Settings › Note names.

Free and Pitchback Full

  • Free: range check, single notes (low, middle, high), five ascending intervals (major 2nd, minor 3rd, major 3rd, perfect 4th, perfect 5th), adaptive review and the pitch monitor.
  • Pitchback Full (one-time, non-consumable in-app purchase): all 24 intervals up and down, opened step by step as you master them, and short melodies (steps, triads and leaps). No subscription. Family Sharing is included.
  • To buy, tap a locked area (lock icon) or Settings › Purchase. The price shown is the App Store's price for your country.
  • Restore: Settings › Purchase › Restore purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Microphone and headphones

  • Wired headphones give the cleanest results. With the speaker, Pitchback starts listening only after the reference has finished. Bluetooth adds delay, and Pitchback waits for it.
  • If the microphone is off, exercises still play but nothing is scored. Turn it on in the Settings app › Pitchback › Microphone.
  • If a result says it did not hear you, sing a little louder or closer to the microphone and hold the note for about a second.

Good to know

  • Pitchback trains pitch matching and intervals by ear. It does not teach reading music and does not promise perfect pitch.
  • Progress and settings are saved on this device only. Deleting the app deletes them.
  • iPhone in portrait; iPad in any orientation. English, Korean, Japanese, German, French, Spanish and Brazilian Portuguese.

한국어

Pitchback은 노래로 답하는 청음 훈련 앱입니다. 음 하나, 음정, 짧은 선율을 듣고 따라 부르면 100점 만점 점수, 몇 센트 높거나 낮았는지, 음이 안정적이었는지 보여 줍니다. 30초 음역 진단으로 시작하고, 틀리는 항목을 더 자주 내 주며, 음이름은 도레미·다라마·C D E 등 익숙한 방식으로 보입니다. 기본 기능은 무료이고 선택형 한 번 구입으로 나머지가 열립니다. 구독·계정·광고는 없습니다.

  • 음역 진단: 처음 실행할 때 ‘계속’ → 마이크 허용 → ‘시작’. 8초 동안 낮은 음에서 높은 음으로 목소리를 올린 뒤 세 음을 따라 부릅니다. 홈의 ‘다시 진단’이나 설정에서 다시 할 수 있습니다.
  • 오늘의 연습: 기준음 뒤 짧은 신호음이 나면 어떤 모음으로든 부르세요. 10센트 이내는 ‘훌륭해요’, 25센트 이내 ‘좋아요’, 50센트 이내 ‘거의 맞았어요’입니다. 소리를 내지 않은 문제는 오답으로 치지 않습니다.
  • 무료: 음역 진단, 단음(저음·중음·고음), 상행 음정 5개(장2도·단3도·장3도·완전4도·완전5도), 맞춤 복습, 음정 모니터.
  • Pitchback 전체 기능(한 번 구입, 비소모성): 상행·하행 24개 음정 전부와 짧은 선율. 구독 없음, 가족 공유 포함. 복원은 설정 › 구입 › ‘구입 복원’. 환불은 Apple(reportaproblem.apple.com)에서 처리합니다.
  • 마이크: 연습·음역 진단·모니터·지연 측정 중에만 켜지고, 소리는 기기 안에서 분석하며 녹음·저장·전송하지 않습니다. 마이크를 끄면 기준음은 들리지만 채점하지 않습니다(설정 앱 › Pitchback › 마이크).
  • 유선 이어폰이 가장 정확합니다. 악보 읽기는 다루지 않으며 절대음감을 약속하지 않습니다. 진도와 설정은 이 기기에만 저장되고 앱을 지우면 함께 지워집니다.
